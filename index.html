A holiday inspiration guide

10 wonderful places to explore in Vietnam

From lantern-lit old towns and mountain air to quiet beaches and river life, find a destination that fits your kind of getaway.

Pick your kind of trip

Vietnam stretches from cool northern highlands to tropical southern shores. These ten destinations offer a mix of culture, scenery, food, and time to unwind.

01

Hạ Long Bay

Drift among limestone karsts, kayak through sheltered coves, or spend a night aboard a boat. A classic choice for dramatic seascapes.

Cruises · Nature Link image for Hạ Long Bay
02

Hội An

Wander a beautifully preserved old town, browse riverside markets, and enjoy lantern-lit evenings. An Cẩm An beach is nearby for a slower day.

Heritage · Food Link image for Hội An
03

Hà Nội

Explore the Old Quarter, lakeside paths, temples, and a lively café scene. It’s also a convenient starting point for trips into northern Vietnam.

Culture · City break Link image for Hà Nội
04

Sa Pa

Take in mountain views, terraced fields, and village trails in the country’s northwest. Pack layers: the highland weather can change quickly.

Mountains · Trekking Link image for Sa Pa
05

Ninh Bình

Take a small-boat ride through limestone scenery and rice fields, then visit temples and viewpoints. It’s often called “Ha Long Bay on land.”

Scenery · Slow travel Link image for Ninh Bình
06

Huế

Discover the former imperial capital’s citadel, royal tombs, and distinctive central Vietnamese cuisine along the Perfume River.

History · Food Link image for Huế
07

Đà Nẵng

Pair a sandy city beach with excellent local food, the Marble Mountains, and easy access to Hội An. A flexible base for central Vietnam.

Beach · City Link image for Da Nang
08

Phong Nha–Kẻ Bàng

Visit a remarkable karst landscape known for caves, rivers, and jungle. Choose from gentle cave visits to more adventurous guided outings.

Caves · Adventure Link image for Phong Nha-Kẻ Bàng
09

Ho Chi Minh City

Experience energetic street life, museums, markets, and a wide range of southern Vietnamese food. It’s a lively gateway to the Mekong Delta.

City life · Food Link image for Ho Chi Minh city
10

Phú Quốc

Slow down on an island getaway with beaches, warm water, and seafood. Check seasonal weather when planning a beach-focused visit.

Island · Relaxation Link image for Phú Quốc

Planning a route?

For a first visit, consider focusing on one region or connecting a few stops by train or domestic flight. Travel times can be longer than the map suggests, so leave room for relaxed days.
